## Add a PivotTable and Slicers (5 minutes, do this yourself)
Pivot tables and slicers cannot be generated by code, so add them in Excel. This is a good thing to show in interviews.

1. Open the file. Go to the **Data** sheet. Click any cell.
2. **Insert > PivotTable > New Worksheet**. Name the sheet `Pivot`.
3. Rows: `Category`. Values: `Sum of Sales`, `Sum of Profit`.
4. **Insert > Slicer**: tick `Region` and `Payment_Mode`. Click a slicer to filter.
5. **PivotTable Analyze > PivotChart** for a clustered column chart.
6. Add a **Timeline** (Insert > Timeline) on `Order_Date`.
7. Optional: Conditional Formatting > Data Bars on the Profit column.
